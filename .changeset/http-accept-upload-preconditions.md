**Breaking:** `acceptUpload` reads the request's `If-Match` and `If-None-Match`, so a `PUT` carrying either is answered `412`, `501` or `204` where v0.6 stored it with `201`, and no option restores that: a caller who means to drop the fields builds a `Request` without them before the call (spec 15, ADR 0075). `If-None-Match: *` is `"absent"`, and an `If-Match` with one strong tag of the form of an `etag` left is `{ etag }`, passed to `put` as `onlyIf`. Before the body is read and without `put`, the first of these that applies answers: a field that is no list of entity tags is `400`; `If-Match: *` or with several tags without a caller's `{ etag }`, `If-None-Match` with tags, `If-Unmodified-Since` without `If-Match`, and a field the storage declares no capability for are `501`; `If-Match` beside `If-None-Match: *` and an `If-Match` with no tag left are `412`. `If-Modified-Since`, `If-Range`, and `If-Unmodified-Since` beside `If-Match` are ignored. A write under `{ etag }` is answered `204` with the `ETag` `put` resolved with, and one without a precondition or under `"absent"` stays `201` (spec 10.5, ADR 0075).
