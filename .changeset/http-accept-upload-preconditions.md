**Breaking:** `acceptUpload` reads the request's `If-Match` and `If-None-Match`, so a `PUT` carrying either is answered `412`, `501` or `204` where v0.6 stored it with `201`, and no option restores that: a caller who means to drop the fields builds a `Request` without them before the call (spec 15, ADR 0075). `AcceptUploadOptions` takes `onlyIf?: Precondition`, the caller's policy, and the layer passes `put` the one `onlyIf` that satisfies it and the request's fields together. `If-None-Match: *` is `"absent"`, and an `If-Match` with one strong tag of the form of an `etag` left is `{ etag }`. Before the body is read and without `put`, a field that is no list of entity tags or an `onlyIf` of the wrong form is `400`; `If-Match: *` or with several tags without a caller's `{ etag }`, `If-None-Match` with tags, `If-Unmodified-Since` without `If-Match`, and a field the storage declares no capability for are `501`; preconditions that cannot hold together and an `If-Match` with no tag left are `412`. `If-Modified-Since` and `If-Range` are ignored. A write under `{ etag }` is answered `204` with the `ETag` `put` resolved with, and one without a precondition or under `"absent"` stays `201`. An `onlyIf` the caller passes on a storage without the capability stays with `put`, answered `500` (spec 10.1, 10.5, ADR 0075).
