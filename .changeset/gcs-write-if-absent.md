`adapter-gcs` declares `writeIfAbsent`. `put`, `copy` and `move` with `onlyIf: "absent"` send `ifGenerationMatch=0` on the `uploadType=multipart` insert, the start of a resumable session, every call of `rewriteTo` and `objects.move`. A key that holds an object rejects with `PreconditionFailed`, `key` set to `to` for `copy` and `move`: GCS answers it `412 conditionNotMet`, which is now `PreconditionFailed` rather than `ProviderError`. A rewrite is refused at the call that commits, so a `copy` that another writer's object overtakes between two calls leaves that object in place, and a `move` its precondition refused leaves both keys as they were. A refusal of the insert, a `rewriteTo` call or `objects.move` after an attempt without a response or with a `5xx` rejects with that earlier failure, since the repeat of a write that went through meets its own object. A resumable session takes no part in that rule: a commit its precondition refused is `PreconditionFailed` and leaves nothing to cancel, and where a commit spent its budget without a response and the cancel answers `412`, `put` rejects with `PreconditionFailed`. `presignPut` binds `"absent"` as the signed header `x-goog-if-generation-match: 0` and returns it in `headers` (ADR 0071, ADR 0073, ADR 0074).
